title What Everyone Has | attributes, things carried, things known

character m | Marlowe | #96e6a0 | -
character p | Peregrine | #c8b4ff | p
character q | Quince | #ffd27f | q
character c | Cook | #ffb070 | c

attribute nerve | Nerve | 1
item coin | Coins | 0
item key | Cellar key | 0 | | opens the wine cellar
info canvas | The fleet is canvas | 0
info recipe | Cook's secret recipe | 0 | hidden | nobody is told when this changes hands
holds m | coin 5
holds q | key | canvas | nerve 3
holds c | recipe | coin 2

*start
bg pantry
show q neutral left
show c neutral right
n: The pantry. Quince has the cellar key and knows something. I have five coins.
choice
- Buy the key from Quince for three coins => buy
- [m.coin >= 9] Buy it for nine => rich
- Ask what Quince knows => ask
*buy
trade m q coin 3 for key 1
m: A fair price.
goto after
*rich
m: I do not have nine coins.
goto after
*ask
copy q m canvas
q: Thirty-one of the sixty hulls are painted canvas. Now we both know.
give m c coin 2
m: And two coins to Cook, for looking the other way.
copy c m recipe
copy q m nerve
*after
trade m q coin 50 for nerve 1
n: (Fifty coins for a little of Quince's nerve: nobody has fifty coins.)
grant p coin 1
give m p key
n: The Young Master comes down for his allowance, and for anything else going.
if p.key >= 1 goto gave
if m.canvas == 1 goto knows
end Nothing learned | And nothing given away.
*gave
end The key goes upstairs | Peregrine has it now.
*knows
end A secret shared | Quince still knows it too.
