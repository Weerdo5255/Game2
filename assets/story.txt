# BELOW STAIRS, ABOVE STARS - story script
#
# Syntax (one command per line):
#   *label                      jump target
#   card Title | Subtitle       chapter card
#   bg name                     change background (clears the cast)
#   show who expr slot          who: p v s q t c a   slot: left center right
#   hide slot|all
#   who: text                   dialogue; "n" is narration, "m" is Marlowe
#   choice                      followed by option lines:
#   - [var >= n] text => label  (the [condition] is optional)
#   add var n / set var n       story variables (all start at 0)
#   if var >= n goto label
#   goto label
#   end Title | Epilogue
#
# Variables: peace (detente), heat (suspicion), favor (Peregrine's regard),
#            trust (Quince), canvas, water, ally (flags)

*start
card Prologue | Thistlemere, Halcyon Reach
bg pantry
n: Morning on Halcyon Reach arrives the way everything arrives here: on schedule, by committee, and three minutes late so that His Lordship may feel he was waited for.
n: The sunline brightens along the axis. Eight kilometres overhead, somebody else's lake catches the light. I stopped looking up years ago. Looking up is for tourists and theologians.
n: My name is Marlowe. I am Head of Household to the Honourable Peregrine Varrow, who turns twenty tomorrow and has never once in his life opened a door.
show c neutral right
c: You've the look of a person counting to ten, Marlowe.
m: I am counting to twenty, Cook. It is the Young Master's birthday.
c: Weather Office rang. His Lordship's had the rain moved again. Says a drizzle on the eve of a gala is 'defeatist'.
c: That's three days of rain banked up now. When they finally let it go, we'll want boats.
m: And our guests?
show c worried right
c: Lord Solberg's yacht matched spin an hour ago. All the way from Meridian Spindle, with forty trunks and, so the dock boys say, his own air.
m: His own air.
c: In bottles. Vintage.
n: Lord Solberg and Lord Varrow have not spoken civilly since the Veil: a comet of excellent ice, parked between our two cylinders and claimed, loudly, by both.
n: Officially, Solberg comes to toast the heir. Unofficially, every pantry between here and Meridian knows what follows a toast when lords are involved. An ultimatum.
n: The bell board chimes. Third row, gold tag. The Axis.
show c smile right
c: He's wanting breakfast up the hub again. Mind the marmalade. It wanders.

card Chapter One | A Breakfast at the Axis
bg axis
n: At the hub of the cylinder there is no gravity, no dignity, and, as of this morning, one croissant in a slow polar orbit around the heir to Thistlemere.
show p smile center

- "Sir, the last weightless course put the Bishop of the Ring in a ventilation duct." => soup_no
