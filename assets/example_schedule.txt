title A Day at Thistlemere | who is where, and when

character m | Marlowe | #96e6a0 | -
character p | Peregrine | #c8b4ff | p
character q | Quince | #ffd27f | q
character c | Cook | #ffb070 | c

clock 08:00 | 5
schedule p | 07:00 axis breakfast | 09:00 vista fencing | 13:00 study
schedule q | 06:00 pantry | 08:20 dock | 12:00 pantry
schedule c | 05:00 pantry | 22:00 away
interrupt c | event fire | vista | flees the kitchen
interrupt q | alarm >= 2 | study | reports upstairs

var alarm | Alarm | 0 | bad

*start
bg pantry
n: Eight o'clock. The pantry.
m: Morning, Quince. Morning, Cook.
m: Quince has the dock at twenty past.
m: And there he goes.
m: Where is the Young Master just now?
if @p.axis == 1 goto at_axis
n: Not at the axis.
goto later
*at_axis
n: At the axis, having Views.
*later
time 09:30
bg vista
n: Half past nine on the terrace.
event fire
m: Something is burning below stairs.
choice
- [@c == 1] Ask Cook what happened => asked
- Say nothing => quiet
*asked
set alarm 2
m: Cook explains. At length.
event fire off
m: The fire is out; Cook goes back down.
bg study
m: Quince is already in the study, reporting.
end Done | A day, more or less.
*quiet
end Quiet | Nothing was said.
