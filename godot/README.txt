Below Stairs, Above Stars

A Godot 4 project, exported from the story editor.

Shows: places as the story sets them (some paintings, some 3D).

Open it with Godot 4.3 or later: start Godot, choose Import, and pick project.godot
in this folder (or: godot --path . --editor). Press F5 to play.

The story is story/story.json, with every scene and choice. Pictures are in art/,
3D places are scenes in places/, sound in audio/, model files in models/.
Edit the pictures and places here in Godot; edit the story in the story editor
and export again (pictures and places changed here are kept unless you ask
for them to be written again).
