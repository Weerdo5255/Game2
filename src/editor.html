Story Editor

Double-click a chapter to open it · drag to arrange · drag a port onto a scene or chapter to reroute · scroll to zoom · double-click empty space for a new scene

How this works

  • Each box is a scene. Lines leaving it are its choices (gold), conditions (blue) and jumps (grey). A dashed line means the scene simply continues into the next one in the Scenes list.
  • A chapter is a group of scenes shown as one panel (double border). The panel lists the ways in, the ways out, and what the chapter passes out: the range of change it can make to each property, and the properties it depends on. Double-click it to open it; the bar at the top left leads back. Put a scene in a chapter from the scene's own panel, or by dragging it from the Scenes list on to the chapter.
  • Select a scene to edit everything in it on the right. Drag blocks by their grip to reorder them, or drop one on another scene in the graph to move it there.
  • Drag the round port at the end of a choice, condition or jump on to another scene to change where it leads.
  • Consequences are the green blocks: they change a property. They show as chips on scenes and beside the choices that lead to them.
  • Cast holds each character's name, colour, portrait and look, and the properties that belong to them. Properties lists every property and where it changes.
  • The story menu at the top switches between stories, and makes, copies or removes them. Each story has its own file, cast and art.
  • Art sends this story's backgrounds and characters through ComfyUI, with a choice of workflows, and shows the results beside the game's own paintings.
  • Sound gives each speaker a voice (a stock voice, or one cloned from a sample recording) and has their lines read out through ComfyUI; it also generates the music tracks that scenes start with a Music block.
  • The story menu's Files group exports the open story as one file (the story alone, or with its pictures and recordings) and imports such a file, or a story text file, as a new story.
  • Manage lists every story with its size and word count, keeps earlier versions of the open story (a snapshot is taken automatically as you save, and you can take named ones), locks a story against changes, and brings removed stories back.
  • Find (Ctrl+F) searches all dialogue, choices, cards and notes, and can replace across the story in one undoable step.
  • Unsaved work is kept in this browser as you go: if the page is closed or crashes, the editor offers it back.
  • Places can be shown as paintings or as a 3D world to walk through: choose for the whole story under Scenes → This story, or place by place there or on any Background block. (The game needs to be built with its 3D engine: --features world3d.)
  • Characters can keep a schedule: open one in Cast and give them times and places, and they are on stage wherever the story is when their day has them there. Interruptions send them elsewhere while an event is on, a property passes a mark, or after a time. The story's clock is under Scenes → This story; Time, Time passes and Event blocks move it and start events; conditions can ask the time, an event, or whether someone is here.
  • Ctrl+S saves to the story file. Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z undo and redo. Play saves first, then starts the game.
